Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about WalletLogs

Is my financial data secure? expand_more

Absolutely! We use bank-level 256-bit encryption and never store your banking credentials. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we comply with all major financial data protection standards.

Can I connect multiple bank accounts? expand_more

Yes! With our Pro and Business plans, you can connect unlimited bank accounts, credit cards, and financial institutions to get a complete view of your finances.

Is there a free trial? expand_more

Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial of our Pro plan. No credit card required. You can cancel anytime during the trial period.

Can I export my data? expand_more

Absolutely! You can export all your transaction data, reports, and insights in multiple formats including CSV, PDF, and Excel at any time.

Do you offer customer support? expand_more

Yes! We provide email support for all users, priority support for Pro users, and dedicated support for Business customers. Our support team typically responds within 24 hours.
